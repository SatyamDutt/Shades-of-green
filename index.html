Project Overview

Where Architecture Meets the Landscape

Shades of Green is envisioned as a low-density luxury residential address where contemporary architecture, private outdoor living and green surroundings come together with quiet sophistication.

The demonstration concept emphasizes generous volumes, natural ventilation, refined material palettes and seamless transitions between indoor and outdoor spaces. Every residence is imagined as a private retreat within a secure, landscaped community.

  • Contemporary architecture with a focus on light, privacy and proportion
  • Landscaped gardens and open spaces designed for everyday wellbeing
  • Premium specifications, finishes and construction standards*
  • A gated community concept with planned security and convenience*

Project Highlights

Designed for Considered, Elevated Living

Each element of the Shades of Green concept is intended to balance architectural presence with comfort, privacy and a strong connection to nature.

Contemporary Architecture

Clean lines, generous glazing and a timeless material palette create villas with a calm, enduring architectural character.

Spacious Residences
